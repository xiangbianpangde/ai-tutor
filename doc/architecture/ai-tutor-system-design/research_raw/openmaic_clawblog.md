## What Is OpenMAIC?

[OpenMAIC](https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC) is a multi-agent classroom simulation from Tsinghua University. Instead of a single AI assistant, OpenMAIC creates a classroom of AI agents that teach, learn from, and challenge each other.

**Core attributes:**

  * **Category** : Multi-agent educational simulation
  * **Origin** : THU-MAIC, Tsinghua University
  * **Agent roles** : Teacher, Student, Reviewer, Moderator
  * **Use cases** : Research training, paper writing practice, concept exploration



* * *

## Agent Roles

Role| Behavior  
---|---  
**Teacher**|  Presents concepts, designs exercises, evaluates understanding  
**Student**|  Asks questions, attempts problems, seeks clarification  
**Reviewer**|  Critiques arguments, checks logic, suggests improvements  
**Moderator**|  Manages discussion flow, ensures balanced participation

## FAQ

### Q1: Is OpenMAIC for educating humans or training AI?

Both. Humans can join as participants alongside AI agents for research training. Researchers can also study the emergent learning behaviors of pure AI classrooms.

### Q2: How does this differ from a chatbot?

A chatbot is one agent responding to one user. OpenMAIC is multiple agents interacting with each other — creating debate, peer review, and teaching dynamics that a single agent cannot produce.

## Summary

OpenMAIC from Tsinghua THU-MAIC brings multi-agent classroom dynamics to research education. Best used for research methodology training and collaborative paper writing practice.
